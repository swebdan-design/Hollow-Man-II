# HOLLOW MAN: THE SECOND SHADOW

### by Synth Centric

*Instruction Manual — Nintendo Game Boy Color*

---

## WARNING — READ BEFORE PLAYING

This is a horror game. It contains violence, a murdered family, corrupt police, and something
that should have stayed buried. Play with the lights on. Or don't.

---

## THE STORY SO FAR

Eleven years ago, seven men broke into a house on **Mercy Street** in the city of **Gloam**.
A family went in. A family did not come out.

Nobody was punished. Evidence vanished. Witnesses forgot what they saw. The case file was
closed by men who were paid to close it.

Somebody took a photograph that night: seven men outside the burning house.
Count them again.

There are **eight**.

Now a man in a **brown tweed suit** and a **black three-hole ski mask** is crossing names off a
list. The papers call him **HOLLOW MAN**. He rarely speaks. He does not stop.

And everywhere he goes, his shadow arrives a moment late — and stays a moment longer.

Detective **Mara Bell** of Gloam Homicide is following the bodies. She does not believe in
ghosts. She believes in fingerprints. She is going to find the end of this trail.

## GETTING STARTED

The game comes in two versions with the same story and controls:

- **Game Boy Advance** — `HOLLOW_MAN_THE_SECOND_SHADOW.gba` (overhauled full-colour graphics).
- **Game Boy Color** — `HOLLOW_MAN_THE_SECOND_SHADOW.gbc` (runs only in Game Boy Color mode).

Insert the Game Pak (or load the ROM in an emulator) and switch the power ON.

At the title screen press **START**. The main menu offers:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **NEW GAME** | Begin the story from the prologue. |
| **CONTINUE** | Resume your last save. Shown dimmed with a lock when no valid save exists. |
| **MUSIC PLAYER** | Listen to the soundtrack. New tracks unlock as you discover them. |
| **OPTIONS** | Music on/off, sound effects on/off, text speed, control reference. |
| **CREDITS** | The people (and things) responsible. |

## CONTROLS

| Button | In the field | In menus |
|---|---|---|
| **D-Pad** | Walk / face a direction | Move cursor |
| **A** | Talk, search, read, open — or use the equipped tool | Confirm |
| **B** | Strike with your melee weapon | Back / cancel |
| **Hold B, release** | Charged heavy strike | — |
| **START** | Pause menu | Close menu |
| **SELECT** | Cycle the equipped tool | — |

**A-button priority:** when you press A, Hollow Man first tries to interact with whatever is
directly in front of him (a person, a sign, a container, a door, a mechanism). Only if there is
nothing to interact with does he use the equipped tool. To throw a bottle or fire the
revolver, face open space.

---

## THE SCREEN

The bar along the bottom of the screen shows:

- **Hearts** — your health. Each heart is two hits of half a heart.
- **Weapon** — PIPE or AXE.
- **Tool** — the tool on SELECT (`---` none, `GUN` revolver, `BTL` bottle, `SHD` shadow,
  `BND` bandage) and its count. During a lockdown this space shows a countdown.
- **The Eye** — the stage of the Second Shadow's influence (closed, half-open, open, bleeding).
- **The Badge** — lit when the police are looking for you.

## MOVEMENT AND EXPLORATION

Gloam is one connected city of seven districts. Walk off the edge of a street along the main
road and you will cross into the neighbouring district. Some routes are chained, barricaded or
locked; you will find the means to open them.

Walk into doors to enter buildings. Press **A** facing anything that looks interesting —
shelves, desks, bins, graves, windows, payphones. Many hide supplies, evidence or secrets.

Some loose crates can be **pushed** by walking into them. If you push one into a corner, simply
leave the room and return: the crates will be back where they started.

---

## COMBAT

Hollow Man fights up close.

- Press **B** to swing your weapon in the direction you face.
- **Hold B** until you hear the cue, then release, for a **heavy strike** that hits twice as hard
  and knocks enemies back. You move slowly while charging.
- After taking a hit, Hollow Man flashes and cannot be hurt for a moment.
- Defeated enemies sometimes drop a **heart** (restores health) or **revolver rounds**.

**Weapons**
- **Lead Pipe** — your starting weapon.
- **Fire Axe** — found later. Twice the damage. Equip it from the Inventory.
- **Revolver** — select it with SELECT, fire with A. Ammunition is scarce, and **every shot
  raises the police alert**.

**Enemies** include knife-carrying thugs, fast evasive runners who strike and retreat, armed
guards and police who keep their distance and shoot when you line up with them, rats in the
dark places, and things in hoods that walk through walls.

---

## STALKING

Most enemies do not know you are there — yet.

- Enemies see in a **cone in front of them**. They cannot see through walls or around corners.
- **Dark floors** (shadowed alleys, unlit corners) shrink how far they can see you.
- A **!** means you have been spotted. A **?** means someone heard something and is searching.
  If they lose you, they search for a while and then return to their route.
- **Strike an unaware enemy from behind** and he goes down instantly.
- **Distractions:** throw an **empty bottle** (SELECT to equip, A to throw) and nearby enemies
  go to investigate the noise. Some objects — payphones, TVs, alarms — make noise when used.
- Being spotted in **restricted** places (shops after hours, the cold storage, the annex...)
  raises the police alert.

---

## POLICE ALERT

The **badge** in the corner tracks how badly Gloam's police want you. Gunshots, being seen in
restricted areas and alarms all raise it. As it rises, patrols appear on the streets. The alert
fades over time, and faster in safe places such as the diner, the bar and friendly homes.

Killing police officers is remembered.

---

## INVESTIGATION AND THE CASEBOOK

Evidence you recover is filed in the **Casebook** (pause menu). The first page tracks progress
on the **seven names**. Choose **FILES** to read every document, photograph, tape and letter you
have found. Evidence opens new places, changes what people tell you, and decides how the story
can end.

Some evidence is hidden. People of Gloam remember Mercy Street — ask them more than once as the
story moves on.

## THE SECOND SHADOW

Something follows Hollow Man. Its hold over him is measured as **Influence** from 0 to 100,
shown as the Eye on screen and in detail under **SHADOW STATUS** in the pause menu.

| Stage | Influence | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Dormant** | 0–24 | Your shadow is only a shadow. Mostly. |
| **Watching** | 25–49 | It lags behind you, drifts on its own. People notice. |
| **Awakened** | 50–74 | It takes a horned shape. The music sours. Once per chapter it will refuse to let you die. |
| **Possession** | 75–100 | It flickers and wears you. Now and then it takes the controls for a moment. |

Influence **rises** when you accept its help, use the **SHADOW** tool, and make certain choices.
It **falls** when you recover evidence, refuse it, and follow the truth instead.

**The SHADOW tool** (once the shadow offers it): equip with SELECT and press A. Enemies near you
are stunned (and at higher stages, hurt). Some walls in Gloam *breathe* — use the shadow on them.
Every use feeds it.

---

## INVENTORY

From the pause menu, **INVENTORY** lists your weapons, key items and supplies.

- Select the **Pipe** or **Axe** to equip it.
- Select **Bandages** to heal (also usable as a SELECT tool).
- Select a **key item** to use it on whatever is in front of you. If nothing there needs it,
  you'll be told so. (Most locks also open automatically when you simply press A at them while
  carrying the right item.)

---

## SAVING YOUR PROGRESS

This Game Pak has battery-backed memory.

- Save at any time from the pause menu (**SAVE**), except during a security lockdown.
- Quiet places — the diner's back phone, a booth at the bar, Mrs. Hale's chair, the archivist's
  desk, the nurses' alcove — also offer to save. The game offers to save after each chapter.
- Your game is written to one of two save slots and then **read back and verified**. The
  previous save is never overwritten until the new one is safe, so switching the power off
  mid-save cannot destroy your progress. A save that fails verification is ignored.
- **CONTINUE** resumes from the newest valid save.

If Hollow Man dies, choose **RETRY** to restart from the moment you entered the current area,
or **QUIT TO TITLE**.

## THE CITY OF GLOAM

| District | |
|---|---|
| **Commercial Strip** | Neon, rain and a pawnshop that buys anything. |
| **Mercy Street** | Where it happened. The house is still there, more or less. |
| **Industrial Quarter** | Warehouses, containers and Vale Cold Storage. |
| **Red Lantern District** | The motel, the bar, the people who rent by the hour. |
| **Old Gloam** | St. Agnes Church, the cemetery and the Police Records Annex. |
| **The Drain** | The storm drains beneath the city. Reached from the canal. |
| **The Last Ward** | The hospital at the edge of town. It closed in 1999. Supposedly. |

Use **CITY MAP** in the pause menu to see the districts you have discovered.

## TIPS

- Talk to everyone. Then talk to them again after each chapter.
- Read signs and notices — every puzzle's answer is written somewhere in Gloam.
- Dark corners are your friends. Bright doorways are not.
- Bosses have patterns. Watch for the moment they reload, stumble or slam into something.
- Bottles are cheap. Bullets are not.
- How the story ends depends on what you let the shadow do — and on how much of the truth you
  dig up. There is more under Mercy Street than ash.
