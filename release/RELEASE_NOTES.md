# HOLLOW MAN: THE SECOND SHADOW
### by Synth Centric — Release 1.1 (Game Boy Advance + Game Boy Color)

A top-down survival-horror detective adventure. A masked man in a brown tweed suit hunts the
seven men behind the Mercy Street killings — while something with horns walks in his shadow.
A standalone game (not a sequel).

## Files
- `HOLLOW_MAN_THE_SECOND_SHADOW.gba` — **Game Boy Advance version** with fully overhauled graphics
- `HOLLOW_MAN_THE_SECOND_SHADOW.gbc` — Game Boy Color version (MBC5 + 8 KB battery RAM, CGB only)
- `HOLLOW_MAN_SOURCE.zip` — complete source; rebuilds both ROMs byte-for-byte (docs/BUILD.md)
- `HOLLOW_MAN_COMPLETE.zip` — both ROMs, manual, docs, release notes, screenshots, source zip
- `screenshots_gba/`, `screenshots/` — genuine emulator captures (mGBA / PyBoy)

## What's new in 1.1
- Renamed to **HOLLOW MAN: THE SECOND SHADOW**.
- **Game Boy Advance version** with all-new art: 16×32 shaded characters with automatic
  outlines, a herringbone-tweed Hollow Man whose knitted balaclava has highlights and visible
  eye/mouth holes, redrawn 15-colour tilesets with proper wall tops, 256-colour title and
  cutscene illustrations where Hollow Man is backlit by fog and streetlights, a wider dialogue
  box and a HUD strip.
- GBC title: a fog halo behind Hollow Man so the black balaclava no longer vanishes into black.

- **Recorded music (GBA):** Synth Centric's own track "Intro" now plays on the GBA title
  screen, streamed through the GBA's sample channel (8-bit, 18 kHz). More tracks can be dropped
  in (see docs/SOUNDTRACK.md).

## Controls (both versions)
D-Pad move · **A** talk/search/use (interaction first, then equipped tool) · **B** strike ·
hold **B** heavy strike · **SELECT** cycle tool · **START** pause menu. (GBA: L/R unused.)

## Features
Seven connected districts and 39 maps; seven playable chapters and bosses (Eddie Voss, Hector
Vale, Nolan Pike, Sgt. Dacre, Alec Mott, Silas Crewe, Victor Rusk); real-time combat; stealth;
the Second Shadow influence system; police alert; Detective Mara Bell; Casebook with 17
entries; puzzles; three endings (NOT TONIGHT, THE EMPTY VESSEL, secret THE EIGHTH MAN); the
original 16-track Synth Centric soundtrack on the Game Boy sound channels (both versions);
Music Player; dual-slot verified battery saves.

## Emulator guidance
GBA: mGBA, VBA-M, NanoBoyAdvance, etc. (save type SRAM 32 KB is auto-detected).
GBC: SameBoy, Emulicious, mGBA, BGB, etc., in Game Boy Color mode.

## Known limitations
- Not tested on real hardware (GBA, GBC or flash carts); tested in mGBA (GBA) and PyBoy (GBC).
- Chapters 2–7 verified by bots, crafted saves and a progression simulator, not a full human
  playthrough; balance tuned against bots.
- Estimated playtime 1.5–3 hours.
- The GBA version plays the recorded "Intro" on the title screen; all other cues still use the
  Game Boy sound channels (same chip soundtrack as the GBC).
- Music not reviewed by ear in this environment (output verified only).
